# YD Play Community Guidelines — Draft

YD Play is intended for fair, respectful, non-real-money entertainment.

Users must not:
- harass, threaten, bully, or target other users;
- post hateful, sexually exploitative, violent, or illegal content;
- impersonate another person or misrepresent an official role;
- spam, scam, phish, or solicit passwords/tokens;
- automate gameplay or use tools that create unfair competitive advantage;
- create/farm accounts or devices to abuse referrals, missions, ads, or rewards;
- exploit bugs, manipulate scores, or interfere with servers;
- evade bans or moderation restrictions.

Reports are reviewed before enforcement. Depending on severity and evidence, actions may include
warnings, mutes, suspensions, bans, reward reversals, or other proportionate restrictions.

Users should have a documented appeal path. Emergency or illegal-content escalation procedures
must be finalized before a public launch.
