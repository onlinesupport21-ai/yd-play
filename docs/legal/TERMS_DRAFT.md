have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, and cannot be exchanged for cash or prizes.

A production launch must define the exact age threshold and supported/blocked regions.

Users may not automate gameplay, tamper with the client or network protocol, exploit bugs,
farm accounts/devices, manipulate referrals or rewards, harass other users, impersonate others,
or attempt unauthorized access.

## Virtual coins
Coins may be granted for gameplay, missions, achievements, referrals, or other in-app activity.
Coins are revocable where obtained through abuse or error. No deposit, wagering, cash-out, or
real-money prize functionality is provided.

appeal path for moderation decisions.

continuous availability is made.

Users retain rights in content they lawfully submit, subject to the permissions required to
operate and moderate the service.

Users may stop using the service at any time. Account deletion/data handling must follow the
published privacy policy and applicable law.
