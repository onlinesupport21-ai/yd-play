# YD Play Privacy Policy — Draft for Legal Review

**Status:** Release-candidate draft. This is not a final privacy policy.

## Data categories
YD Play may process account/profile data, authentication/session data, virtual-wallet ledger
records, game sessions/results, referral relationships, missions/achievements, moderation
reports, notifications, analytics events, and security/abuse signals.

## Device and abuse prevention
The client is designed to provide a privacy-preserving device proof which the server hashes for
abuse prevention. Production policy must accurately describe all device/IP/security signals
actually collected and their retention.

## Purposes
Data is used to authenticate users, operate games, maintain the virtual coin ledger, detect
fraud/cheating, deliver notifications, moderate community activity, troubleshoot reliability,
and measure service performance.

## Sharing
The final policy must name or categorize production service providers such as hosting,
database, analytics, notification, email, crash reporting, or support providers if they are used.
YD Play should not claim a provider is used until it is actually configured.

## Retention
A production retention schedule must define how long account, ledger, security, moderation,
analytics, and backup data are retained.

## User controls
The production service must provide applicable access, correction, deletion, portability,
notification, and consent controls required by the launch jurisdiction.

## Security
Traffic should use HTTPS/WSS. Secrets and tokens must be protected, admin access must use
RBAC/MFA where available, and sensitive production data should be encrypted appropriately.

## Children / age gate
The final policy must reflect the actual minimum age and handling of underage users for each
supported region.
